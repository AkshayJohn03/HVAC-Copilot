03 · chunking

Two librarians, one fused list

BM25 — the keyword librarian
1 · “E04” fault-table row 2 · coil cleaning procedure 3 · manual overview
Dense retrieval — the meaning librarian
1 · low cooling capacity 2 · coil frost / defrost 3 · manual overview
field slang “not cooling” lands near “low cooling capacity” — zero shared words, same meaning
RRF: score = Σ 1 / (60 + rank)
→
1 · “E04” fault-table row ✓ 2 · low cooling capacity
BM25 Keyword ranking: scores chunks by how often your exact words appear, weighted by how rare they are.
dense retrieval Matches meaning, not words — so typos and field slang still find the right section.
embedding · vector Every chunk becomes a vector: a list of numbers representing what the text means.
cosine similarity The similarity of two vectors is the angle between them — 1.0 is the same direction, 0.0 unrelated.
RRF — reciprocal rank fusion Merge two ranked lists: a chunk scores for every list it appears in. No score calibration needed.
hybrid retrieval Keyword for precision, meaning for slang — fused into one list.

groundedness check: every word — and every number — in the answer must exist in the retrieved pages. If they don’t, the system says what’s missing instead of guessing.
citation The answer names the document, chapter and section it used — derived from what it actually used.
groundedness How much of the answer is backed by retrieved context — measured, deterministically, no judge model.
06 · citations

The safety refusal

“How much refrigerant does the unit carry?”
SPEC LOOKUP — EXEMPT
Naming a factory charge weight synthesizes nothing → answered from the spec table: “factory charge 1.35 kg”.
“Recover the refrigerant.”
WORK VERB → SAFETY-CRITICAL
Same noun, now a work verb — hazardous work on pressurized circuits.
“I can’t give procedural guidance for this. The question involves refrigerant for the X200, which is safety-critical, and no section tagged as safety documentation supports an answer here. Stop and contact a certified HVAC technician; a supervisor has been flagged to review this request.”
cites the nearest safety section: “3.4 Annually — refrigerant circuit and electrical inspection”
safety-critical topic Refrigerant handling, pressurized systems, live electrical work — where wrong guidance can hurt someone.
two-tier detection A designation counts only with a work verb: “R32” alone is fine — “recover the R32” is not.
retrieval-grounded refusal Computed from the index — no safety-tagged source, no guidance. Deterministic, testable, auditable.
alarm fatigue A guardrail that fires on harmless questions trains users to ignore it — hence the exemption.
